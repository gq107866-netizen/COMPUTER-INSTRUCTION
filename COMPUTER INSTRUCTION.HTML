Computer Instructions

A Beginner's Guide to Using a Computer

Basic Computer Steps

  1. Connect the computer to a power supply.
  2. Turn on the power button.
  3. Wait for the operating system to load.
  4. Enter your password if required.
  5. Use the mouse and keyboard to work.
  6. Open programs such as a web browser or Notepad.
  7. Save your work when necessary.
  8. Close your programs when finished.
  9. Shut down the computer using the system menu.

Computer Safety Tips

  • Keep food and drinks away from the computer.
  • Do not share your password.
  • Avoid downloading unknown files.
  • Save your work regularly.
